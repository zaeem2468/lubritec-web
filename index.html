How To Order

Requesting quotes is easy

Note: This platform is strictly for B2B traders and bulk purchases.

1

Create an Account

Our wholesale portal requires registration. Click the Login / Register Now button below to create your client profile. Only logged-in users can browse products and build order baskets.

2

Build Your Basket

Once logged in, navigate to our Product Catalog. Select your required case loads, type in the quantity, and add them directly to your digital basket.

3

Receive Pricing & Submit

Our admin team will load your uniquely negotiated case prices directly into your account. Review your basket, enter your delivery details, and submit your RFQ or send it directly via WhatsApp.
