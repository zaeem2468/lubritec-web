About Route 64

Protect What Matters, Lubricate What Moves

Route 64 Lubricants is the leading provider of high-performance, API-certified oils, greases, and industrial chemicals in the Gauteng region. Based out of Aureus, Randfontein, we supply to a wide array of demanding sectors.
