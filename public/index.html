How to order

Route 64 sells to trade and bulk buyers only. Quotes take three steps.

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Register with your business email. You need an account to see the catalogue and build a basket.

  2. 2

    Build your basket

    Choose a product, pick the pack size, enter the number of cases and add it to your basket.

  3. 3

    Get your price and submit

    Our team loads the prices negotiated for your account. Add your delivery details, then download your quote or send it on WhatsApp.
